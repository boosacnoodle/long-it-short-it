# Free promo playbook — REKTcity "Long It Short It"

Budget: £0. Everything below costs nothing but time.

## Assets built (assets/)
- `rektcity-vertical-15s.mp4` — 1080×1920, 15s, crossfaded 3 beats, audio from the
  loudest window of the master (80s mark). Ready for Reels / TikTok / Shorts.
- `card-1-still-rekt.jpg`, `card-2-down-bad.jpg`, `card-3-the-trade.jpg` — 1080×1080
  hook cards for X, IG feed, Facebook.
- `build_assets.py` — regenerates everything from the master WAV + Spotify artwork.

## Landing page (free hosting, free forever)
https://boosacnoodle.github.io/long-it-short-it/

## Day 1 — post while the market is still red (timing > polish)
X: post the video with hook 1, then reply to your own post with the link.
   "BTC -5.4%, ETH -7.5%, my portfolio -100%. Anyway, new song out."
Reddit r/SatoshiStreetBets + r/CryptoMoonShots: video + one line, link in comment.
   Do NOT use r/wallstreetbets for a first post — new accounts get insta-removed.
TikTok: upload the video, caption "POV: you bought the dip three dips ago",
   hashtags #cryptotok #downbad #degens #cryptoloss #rekt.
IG Reels: same video, cross-post. Add card-1 as the cover.

## Day 2 — contexts, not ads
Reply under liquidation / "worst day" threads on X with a red candle screenshot
and the video. Value first, link second.
Discord / Telegram trading groups: ask a mod, post the 15s clip (not a link).

## Day 3-7 — free playlist + curator routes
- Spotify for Artists: upload a Canvas (8s looping video) — free, lifts save rate.
- Daily Playlists / SoundPlate / Musosoup free tiers: submit to degen/trap/hustle playlists.
- Every friend who streams: ask for a save + a story share, not a "listen pls".

## What money would add later (not now)
£50 Reddit Ads, £50 Meta Ads, ~£40 SubmitHub pitches. Hold until organic proves a hook.

## Rules that keep this from being spam
- Never bare-link into a subreddit.
- Never mass-tag accounts.
- One post per community per week.
- Reply to every comment for the first hour — the algorithm reads reply velocity.
